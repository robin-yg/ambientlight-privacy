Ambient Light – Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Ambient Light (package com.niborgy.ambientlight) controls Bluetooth ambient LED controllers in your car. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect

None. The app doesn't have an account, doesn't use analytics or advertising, and has no internet permission, so it can't send any information off your device.

Information stored on your device

The app saves its settings on your device only: the Bluetooth address of the lamp controller you connected to, your chosen colours, brightness, effect and speed, and options such as auto-connect. This information is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth – to find and connect to your lamp controller and send it colour and effect commands.
  • Location (Android 11 and older only) – Android requires this permission to scan for Bluetooth devices on these versions. The app doesn't read or store your location.

Android Auto

On Android Auto the app shows lighting controls on your car's screen. It doesn't access vehicle data.

Sharing

We don't share information with anyone, because we don't collect any.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
